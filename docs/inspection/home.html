Meet our specialized immigration law firm

a growing firm

My Nomad Lawyer is a growing firm combining international experience, deep expertise in immigration and investment law, together with a fully personalized approach.

The firm is led by Gemma Bueno, a top-tier immigration attorney in Spain and recognized immigration lawyer for moving to Spain, who also serves on the Immigration Subcommittee of the Málaga Bar Association. We provide efficient, reliable, and client-oriented solutions for digital nomads and foreign investors.

Gemma holds the CCBE European Lawyer’s Card, issued by the Council of Bars and Law Societies of Europe. This certifies her as a recognized lawyer in all EU member states, enabling her to practice before European courts and institutions while ensuring legal services that meet the highest European standards.
